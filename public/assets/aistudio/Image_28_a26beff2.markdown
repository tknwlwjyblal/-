## Brand & Style

This design system embodies trust, prestige, communal solidarity, and deep-rooted Yemeni cultural heritage. Built for a peer-to-peer rotating savings and credit association (ROSCA / الجمعيات المالية والادخارية), the UI evokes the warmth of Yemeni architectural mudbrick towers (Shibam / Sana'a Qamariyah motifs) blended with modern fintech sophistication.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Trustworthy & Grounded:** Cultivates security and transparency around money management among families and trusted circles.
- **Warm & Prestigious:** Rich golden bronze accents paired with deep earthen teal-slate and sunlit sand backdrops convey quiet luxury without feeling alienating.
- **Communal & Welcoming:** Emphasizes shared prosperity, seamless collaboration, and clarity of collective contributions.

### Design Style: Heritage Fintech Luxury
A refined blend of **Modern Warm Minimalism** and **Tactile Architectural Motifs**:
- Warm linen/sand canvas textures (`#F9F6F0`, `#F3ECE2`) evoking raw clay, plaster, and natural paper.
- Deep petrol slate (`#1E2B2B`) provides authoritative, ultra-crisp typography and foundational structure.
- Warm Yemeni bronze and brushed ochre gold (`#C89D58`, `#8C6239`) anchor primary interactive states, key metrics, and milestone celebrations.
- Subtle geometric Qamariyah (قمريات) filigree and chevron masonry patterns act as structural dividers and card watermarks at low opacities (3% - 6%).

## Colors

The palette directly mirrors the warm terracotta clay, carved plaster reliefs, and deep bronze-gold emblem hues established in the brand identity:

- **Primary (`#8C6239` - Yemeni Bronze / Terracotta):** Represents the rich earth, clay craftsmanship, and primary calls to action, active tabs, and critical financial milestones.
- **Secondary (`#1E2B2B` - Deep Petrol Slate):** Imparts solemn financial authority, high-contrast readability, and deep anchor framing. Used for primary typography, dark pill badges, and prominent header surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#C89D58` - Brushed Sana'ani Gold):** Denotes accrued dividends, active payout turns, celebration states, and premium highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F9F6F0` Base, `#F3ECE2` Surface Layer 1, `#E8DECE` Surface Layer 2):** Soft, warm sandstone background providing an authentic, non-sterile atmosphere that reduces eye fatigue while elevating warmth.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - *Success (Al-Khayr Green):* `#2D6A4F` for successful deposits, on-time payments, and completed pools.
  - *Warning (Amber Ochre):* `#D97706` for approaching due dates.
  - *Error / Alert (Crimson Earth):* `#9F2B2B` for overdue installments or pool delays.

## Typography

The type hierarchy is tailored for Right-to-Left (RTL) Arabic typography (utilizing Plus Jakarta Sans alongside Arabic native fallbacks such as Cairo, Tajawal, or Readex Pro). 

- **Display & Headings:** Confident, robust, and sculptured. Numbers are formatted with high legibility for currency amounts (YER / SAR / USD) and turn positions.
- **Body & Metrics:** Balanced line heights ensure Arabic diacritics and letters with descending tails (like ح, ج, ع, and ي) do not collide or clip.
- **Numbers:** Currency and countdown trackers always use tabular numerals for perfect vertical alignment in financial logs and installment tables.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first layout prioritizing one-handed ease of use for financial actions and member status auditing:

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Mobile (320px - 599px): 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters.
  - Tablet (600px - 1023px): 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins.
  - Desktop / Web Dashboard (1024px+): 12-column fixed-max layout capped at 1200px width with automatic centering.
- **Financial Cards Rhythm:** Savings round overview cards utilize `space-lg` inner padding to maintain breathing room around aggregate sums, avatars, and progress rings.
- **RTL Fluidity:** All margins, paddings, and alignment orient naturally to right-to-left layout trees, with primary progress flows progressing right-to-left.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines warm earthen surface tiers with soft bronze-tinted ambient lighting:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Base Canvas:* `#F9F6F0` (warm gypsum stone).
  - *Tier 1 (Cards, Modals, Action Sheets):* Pure `#FFFFFF` or `#FDFCF9` with micro-borders of `#E8DECE`.
  - *Tier 2 (Elevated Badges & Inset Fields):* `#F3ECE2`.
  - *Heritage Accent Tier (Active Pool Banner):* Deep Petrol Slate `#1E2B2B` with metallic gold filigree outlines.
- **Shadows:** 
  - *Standard Card Shadow:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(92, 61, 46, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 43, 43, 0.04)`.
  - *Floating Action Button / Payment Modal:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(140, 98, 57, 0.22)`.
  - Never use cold gray or stark black drop-shadows; all shadow hues are warmed with deep terra-umber tones.
- **Ghost Outlines:** Structural borders use subtle `#E8DECE` at 1px thickness, producing clean boundaries without harsh visual weight.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances contemporary UI conventions with the soft architectural arches seen in traditional Yemeni towers and the circular camaraderie of the logo:

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Metric Badges):** `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px).
- **Hero Savings Cards & Bottom Sheets:** `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) providing an organic, clay-sculpted feel.
- **Avatars & Member Turn Indicators:** Circular (`rounded-full`) referencing the circular fellowship motif from the brand emblem.
- **Traditional Arch Accent:** Feature headers and modal tops can optionally carry an arched decorative apex inspired by Sana'ani windows (Qamariya).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Payment, Join Pool, Create الجمعية):** Deep Bronze gradient (`#8C6239` to `#75502C`) with crisp white typography, subtle top inner-bevel glow, and `rounded-lg` radius. Height is 48px to 54px for comfortable touch targets.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with a 1.5px border in `#1E2B2B` and `#1E2B2B` typography.
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Warm sand `#F3ECE2` fill with `#8C6239` text.

### Cards (The Core "Savings Circle" / الجمعية Card)
- Surface in crisp `#FFFFFF` framed by a fine `1px solid #E8DECE` border.
- Header highlights the pool title, current round ("الدور الرابع من 10"), and monthly due amount.
- Progress track features a warm golden bronze bar (`#C89D58`) on a muted sand rail (`#EDE4D6`).
- Bottom area houses overlapping circular avatars of contributing members, with gold halos highlighting whose turn it is to receive the payout ("المستلم هذا الشهر").

### Input Fields & Amount Selectors
- Background filled with `#FDFBF8`, border in `#E0D5C1`, transitioning on focus to `#8C6239` with a subtle 3px ring (`rgba(140, 98, 57, 0.15)`).
- Prefix and suffix labels (e.g., "ر.ي" or "ر.س") set in `#8C6239` bold tabular font.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Paid / تم الدفع:** Soft sage background (`rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.12)`) with `#2D6A4F` text.
- **Current Turn / دورك الآن:** Radiant gold background (`rgba(200, 157, 88, 0.18)`) with `#8C6239` bold text.
- **Upcoming / قادم:** Neutral slate background (`rgba(30, 43, 43, 0.08)`) with `#1E2B2B` text.

### Member Timeline & Turn Tracker
- Vertical and horizontal circular step trackers resembling traditional tribal coin rings or prayer beads.
- Active recipient turn is encased in an ornamental gold-embossed ring.
